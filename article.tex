\section{Introduction}
Number theory studies the properties
of integers.

\section{An Example}
Consider the equation
\[
x^2 - 1 = 0.
\]
Factoring gives
\[
(x-1)(x+1)=0.
\]
Therefore, \(x=1\) or \(x=-1\).

\section{Conclusion}
Factoring is a useful technique
for solving polynomial equations.
